When did you last hear her voice?

You meant to call Sunday. Then the week happened. You know she's alone in that house — that most days, the phone doesn't ring at all, and she'd never tell you how quiet it gets. She doesn't want to be a burden. So she says she's fine.

Meanwhile you're left wondering. Did she eat today? Did he take his pills? And underneath it, the worry you don't say out loud: if something happened, would I even know?

You can't call every morning. But someone can — someone who listens, remembers, and tells you how she's really doing.

Aunt Mabel is there on the days you can't be. Loneliness ends tomorrow morning at nine.

How it works

Three small steps, and then it simply happens every morning.

1

Sign up your loved one

Their name, their phone number, and the time of day they'd most like to hear from someone. That's it — nothing for them to set up, download, or learn.

2

Mabel calls them every day

A real conversation, never a robotic script. She asks about their morning, remembers what they told her yesterday, and lets them talk as long as they'd like.

3

You stay in the loop

A warm summary of every call lands in your family dashboard. Good days are good days — and if something's wrong, you hear about it right away.

She reaches you the moment it matters.

If your loved one is ever in distress — a fall, chest pain, confusion, a scare — Mabel doesn't wait until tomorrow. She urges them to call 911, and in the same breath she reaches you, right then, bringing you onto the call live.

So a frightened parent is never alone — and you're there in seconds.

A knock on the door when disaster strikes

Built with the Cajun Navy Ground Force. When a storm or disaster hits their area, the daily call becomes an in-person check — a commitment to try, from people who have spent years showing up.

What Aunt Mabel is — and what she isn't.

Aunt Mabel is a friendly daily check-in and a fast way to reach family. She is not an emergency service, a medical device, or a monitoring system, and she is not a substitute for care or for calling for help.
